Privacy Policy

Muscleify AI · last updated 12 October 2026

Muscleify AI ("the app") is a workout tracker that counts reps and checks your form with your phone's camera. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Email addressTo create your account and send you a sign-in codeOur account provider (Supabase)
Apple or Google account ID (if you sign in with Apple or Google)To sign you in. With Apple you can choose to hide your real emailSupabase
Your workout data: sets, reps, weights, workouts, routines, custom exercises, settingsTo save your training and restore it on a new phoneOn your phone, and a copy in your account at Supabase

What we don't collect

  • Camera video and images never leave your phone. Rep counting and form checks run on the device; nothing is recorded, uploaded or stored.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and workout data, and sends sign-in emails through Resend.
  • GitHub hosts the exercise pictures. When a picture loads, GitHub sees your device's internet (IP) address, as with any website.
  • Apple / Google, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • RevenueCat checks your App Store purchase so we can unlock paid features. It gets the purchase record and an anonymous app ID, never your card details.

Each of these processes data only to provide its service to us.

Purchases

Some features are unlocked by a one-time purchase, bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment: we never see or store your card or billing details. We only learn whether the purchase was made, so we can unlock the paid features. You can restore it on a new device with Restore purchases.

How long we keep it

Your data stays in your account until you delete it. Signing out removes it from the phone, and it stays in your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → ⚙ Settings → Your data → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all data saved to it, from the cloud and from the phone. You can also email us (below) and we will delete it for you.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each person can only read and change their own data.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
